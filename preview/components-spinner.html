shape
A masked ring rendered from a conic gradient (transparent → currentColor over 360°), with a round "head" dot at the seam. Ring thickness controlled by --bk-spin-thick.
motion
Single 1.1s linear rotation. The taper itself is static within the spinner — the gradient does the work, so motion stays smooth and one-speed. Respects prefers-reduced-motion (slows to 2.8s).
sizes
xs 12 · sm 16 · md 24 (default) · lg 40 · xl 64. Stroke widens at xs/sm for visibility.
color
Inherits currentColor — set color on the parent. Variants (--brand · --ai · --success · --warning · --danger · --subtle · --inverse) are shortcuts to common values.
which color
Blue-600 (--brand, the default) for all loading, including every field and popover in the Select family. Reach for another tone only when the spinner sits on a surface that already carries it: --inverse on dark chrome, --ai on AI Assistant surfaces, or a status pill's own tone via currentColor. Never tint a loading spinner cyan. Cyan is the focus color (--ring-focus and the focus border are both Cyan-600), so a cyan spinner inside an open, focused control double-encodes and erodes what cyan means. Blue-600 also carries 8.7:1 on white against Cyan-600's 4.7:1, which the 1px ring needs since the comet tail fades to transparent. --cyan is deprecated for this reason.
placement
In button: sm size, replaces or precedes label. Disable button while loading. In field: sm trailing, replaces icon. Block: lg/xl centered with label text below for full-section loads.
a11y
Wrap in role="status" aria-live="polite" with an aria-label describing what's loading. Don't rely on visual motion alone — screen readers need text.
vs. skeleton
Use a Spinner for short, indeterminate waits (<3s) and inline contexts. Use Skeleton loader for layout-shaped loads where you can preview the content's structure.
